## Corrections and unverified statements

- Supplier case studies, captions and brochures are now expressly described as statements by the supplier or publisher until independently corroborated.
- Patent publications are no longer phrased as proof of implemented technology or operating capability.
- Image metadata is no longer allowed to imply authenticity; it is described as contextual information that itself requires verification.
- Analytical confidence is distinguished from probability and tied to the information base and analytical rigour.
- The data-minimisation statement applies when personal data are processed and does not purport to decide the full legal position of a specific engagement.
- No customer, result, project, production-capacity or certification claim was added.
- The articles still contain hypothetical industrial examples. They are explanatory examples, not claims about a named company or completed MelonCactus engagement.

No material claim was found that required removal for lack of support after the wording corrections above. The precise applicability of legal and professional guidance to a future client assignment remains fact-dependent.
