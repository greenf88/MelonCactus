### Full proposed retention passage — English (not yet approved)

> We keep enquiries that do not become assignments, and correspondence about them, in our active business mailbox and working files for no more than twelve months after the last substantive contact, and for less time when follow-up is no longer needed. If an enquiry becomes an assignment, subsequent assignment correspondence follows a separate period. It runs from written scope confirmation until no later than 24 months after final delivery, or less time when handover and any corrections are complete. We keep research working files for no more than 24 months after final delivery, and for less time when they are no longer needed. We keep the final report for no more than 24 months from final delivery, or less time when the need for corrections or contractual resolution ends earlier. Where a concrete legal claim or statutory duty requires longer retention, we limit this to the relevant records and review them when that claim or duty ends. We keep scope confirmations, invoices and other necessary basic financial records for the applicable statutory period, generally seven years in the Netherlands after a record ceases to have current administrative value; this does not turn the whole research dossier into fiscal administration. These periods for our active copies do not guarantee that provider-held copies or security records disappear at the same time. Different deletion processes, backup cycles and legal exceptions may apply there. Resend describes a routine 30-day period for email content and delivery logs on its Free, Pro and Scale plans; that is not the period for our business mailbox. Vercel says an Analytics visitor session expires after 24 hours. This is not a general deletion period for raw request data, aggregate statistics or hosting data; the dashboard reporting window is not a deletion guarantee either.

The policy is approved and a refined version is now in the **draft** privacy pages, but the inventory and review have not been observed. The active-copy limits must not be approved for publication as an operational assurance until Rick has addressed that gap. No automatic deletion or monthly operation is claimed as existing.

## Web Analytics: supplier periods and objection

| Data or process | Substantiated on 5 October 2026 | Not substantiated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pro dashboard reporting window | Vercel documents **12 months**; Plus documents 24 months. The 3 October dashboard showed Plus not active. | A maximum deletion date: Vercel explicitly says data may be retained longer than the reporting window. |
| Visitor hash/session | Vercel describes a request-derived hash valid for one day and a visitor session discarded after 24 hours. | The exact hash inputs, deletion time of every derived/raw value, and a 24-hour period for all event data. |
| Event and aggregate data | Vercel describes pageview data points and aggregate dashboard results. | Separate maximum retention for raw request/event data, aggregate statistics and backups for this project. The DPA supplies broad service/termination criteria, not these product-specific maxima. |
| Individual history | Vercel says dashboard data are aggregated and not linked to an identifiable visitor. | A supported lookup or deletion of one person's past Analytics events. Do not demand an IP or fingerprint solely to attempt a lookup. |

The Vercel Web Analytics documentation inspected on **5 October 2026** did not identify a built-in per-visitor opt-out or individual historical deletion control. That is an absence in the documentation inspected, **not** proof that no private support mechanism exists. The site's current `beforeSend` filter can return `null` to suppress an event, but it has no visitor preference or email-to-browser linkage. An email alone therefore cannot reliably stop future pageviews from that person's browser. Historical aggregate dashboard entries cannot honestly be promised as individually findable or erasable.

**Owner choice 2 approved on 6 October:** implement and test a visible browser-bound Analytics opt-out on this preview, storing only an on/off preference, with no retrospective visitor identifier and a deliberate way to turn measurement on again. It applies only to this browser/device, may be lost when browser data are cleared, and does not identify or erase past aggregate statistics. Rick can receive objections by email and explain the browser control; do not ask for IP, account or fingerprint merely to find an Analytics visit. The code is in this draft PR; **actual preview network verification must be recorded before claiming that it works**. If it cannot be made effective, a separately approved consent-before-load or Analytics-off decision remains necessary.

## Consent assessment and supplier question

The observed no-Plus/no-Drains/no-visible-custom-events setup, site-side known-route pageview filter and two clean preview requests materially strengthen the *limited analytics* case. They do not establish all production referrers, headers, cookies or storage. A legitimate-interest basis under Article 6(1)(f) and the separate low-impact device-access exception under Article 11.7a Telecommunicatiewet remain **conditional**, especially on effective objection and the real impact of referrer/IP/device data. The unknown `vercel-feature-flags` writer alone is not proof of an Analytics leak or a reason to block publication. No consent/cookie-banner verdict is inferred solely from Vercel's cookie claim.

**Question prepared for Vercel; do not send without a separate request:** For the MelonCactus Pro Web Analytics project without Plus or Drains, please distinguish (a) how long the one-day visitor hash or session identifier is held; (b) whether raw request IP, full referrer URL including query strings, event records and derived device/geolocation data are persisted and their maximum retention; (c) the retention/deletion of aggregated statistics and any backups, separately from the 12-month dashboard reporting window; and (d) whether a customer can suppress future pageviews for one browser or locate/delete historical data for one person, without introducing a new identifier. Please identify the applicable documentation or contractual term for each answer. Do not include any visitor data in this request.
