## Sources checked on 3 October 2026

- [GDPR Articles 5, 13 and 14 (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679): storage limitation, information on retention/basis/recipients/transfers, and indirectly obtained information. Exact application to a research assignment remains case-specific.
- [Dutch Data Protection Authority: retention](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/nl/over-privacy/persoonsgegevens/bewaren-van-persoonsgegevens), [cookies](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/nl/onderwerpen/internet-telefoon-tv-en-post/cookies), [clear cookie banners](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/themas/internet-slimme-apparaten/cookies/heldere-cookiebanners) and [right to information](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/nl/zelf-doen/privacyrechten/recht-op-informatie): regulator explanations. The currently observed preview evidence is insufficient to conclusively classify every production request/storage action.
- [Resend DPA](https://resend.com/legal/dpa) and [Resend GDPR information](https://resend.com/security/gdpr): supplier text, not independent proof of the current production secret or an account-specific signed copy.
- [Vercel Web Analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics), [Analytics privacy](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy) and [Vercel DPA](https://vercel.com/legal/dpa): supplier text, not a complete production-network measurement or a legal opinion on consent.
- [RVO/Ondernemersplein fiscal administration](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/administratie-bijhouden-en-bewaren/): generally seven years for the **basic fiscal records**, not every research working file or report.
