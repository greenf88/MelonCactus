## Short internal lawful-basis assessment

This is a preliminary purpose/necessity/balancing record, **not** a blanket approval of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The [Dutch Data Protection Authority's explanation of grounds](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/themas/basis-avg/avg-algemeen/grondslagen-avg-uitgelegd) requires an identifiable legitimate interest, necessity and a balance with the individual's interests. Objections and information rights remain applicable.

| Processing | Proposed basis and purpose | Necessity, balance and safeguards | Open point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enquiry from an individual who personally requests/contracts | Article 6(1)(b): steps at their request before contract or contract performance | Only contact, decision/scope and delivery details needed for the requested response; no marketing opt-in; 12-month active limit if no assignment. | Confirm each actual contract-party situation. |
| B2B contact and correspondence | Article 6(1)(f): respond to an authorised business enquiry and manage the relationship | Contacting a work representative is foreseeable, but role/context fields can still be sensitive in combination; collect only relevant data, limit access, avoid marketing reuse and honour objection. | Owner/adviser to sign off balancing and deletion operation. |
| Assignment administration | Individual: Article 6(1)(b) where necessary. Company representatives: Article 6(1)(f). Necessary fiscal records: Article 6(1)(c). | Restrict project and invoice records to the agreed assignment and legal accounting need; separate research files from fiscal records. | Confirm record types and contract-party wording. |
| Hosting/security | Article 6(1)(f): deliver and protect the website | Technical request/security data is necessary for availability and abuse prevention; avoid unnecessary disclosure and apply vendor controls. | Review actual Vercel logs, retention and transfer terms. |
| Limited Web Analytics | Article 6(1)(f) provisionally: understand aggregate use of public routes | Low-impact design: known-route allowlist, URL query/fragment removal, no custom events or advertising profiles; provider describes a short-lived visitor hash. The owner-shown preview POSTs support the filter on two contact pages. Visitors may still have an interest in not being measured; no-cookie alone is not the legal test. | Check production requests, storage/cookies and actual vendor configuration; decide consent under applicable rules on the observed processing, not a generic vendor claim. |
| Research involving people named in public sources | **Per-assignment decision only**; a legitimate interest cannot be presumed for all projects | Concrete decision purpose, necessity, data minimisation, context, reasonable expectations, potential impact and safeguards must be balanced. Determine controller/processor roles and Article 14 duties, with no blanket exemption. | Record each assessment before processing. |
