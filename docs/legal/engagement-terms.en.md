# Draft terms for business research engagements — English

**Version 1 October 2026 · for substantive and legal review only · not applicable until GFNI provides them before engagement and both parties agree to their application in writing.** Website terms govern website use only. This draft deliberately sets no final payment period, cancellation charge, liability cap or dispute forum; these choices appear in the review list and must not be inferred from this draft.

1. **Parties and audience.** MelonCactus is operated by GFNI, Dutch Chamber of Commerce number 93879695 (owner-provided; formal register verification outstanding). The other party is the business client named in the scope confirmation. This is not a consumer checkout.
2. **Formation and order of precedence.** A form submission, call or proposal is not an accepted engagement. The engagement starts only after written agreement on the decision question, scope, deliverables, fee, schedule and applicable terms. The individual scope confirmation prevails over these terms if they conflict. A separately signed NDA prevails over general confidentiality terms but changes scope, fee or timing only if expressly agreed.
3. **Scope and changes.** The confirmation describes questions, geographic and technical boundaries, exclusions, outputs and file formats. Additional questions, sources or outputs require a written adjustment of fee and timing. Research is limited to lawfully accessible public sources and information lawfully supplied by the client. It excludes private account or system access, penetration tests, unlimited monitoring and legal certification.
4. **Effort and evidence limits.** GFNI undertakes careful professional effort, not a guarantee of a commercial outcome, complete knowledge of a competitor or a predetermined conclusion. Reports distinguish observations, supplier claims, assessments, inferences and unknowns. Sources may change; findings are bounded by the agreed research date and evidence limits.
5. **Client cooperation.** The client provides agreed information, a contact person and any necessary permission for its own material in time. The client remains responsible for the lawfulness of supplied information and its own decisions based on the report.
6. **Timing and urgent work.** The deadline and fixed fee appear in the written confirmation. Accelerated timing becomes binding only after written confirmation of scope, fee and deadline and receipt of all required material. Capacity, scope and responsible verification determine availability; evidence and quality standards do not change. Missing client information or a written scope change requires a revised schedule.
7. **Fee, VAT and payment.** The confirmation states the fixed fee, applicable VAT treatment, invoice point and payment period. Website prices are indications or a stated fixed entry fee, not automatic orders. Tax status and payment rules are not yet confirmed and must be decided before this draft is used. Consequences of late payment will be addressed in the approved version; this draft creates no separate collection charge.
8. **Cancellation, suspension and termination.** Before work starts, the parties define when work may be cancelled or suspended and how demonstrably completed work and unavoidable costs will be settled. A material breach should be notified in writing with a reasonable opportunity to remedy where possible. Obligations that by their nature continue, such as confidentiality and payment due for agreed work, survive. Exact cancellation costs remain a commercial decision.
9. **Confidentiality and personal data.** The parties use confidential project information only for the engagement and share it only with people or providers who need it and are appropriately bound. Disclosure required by law remains possible. GDPR roles and arrangements are assessed per engagement; public availability does not remove personal-data protection. An NDA or data-processing agreement is agreed separately where needed.
10. **Third parties and source rights.** GFNI may use necessary hosting, email and other agreed tools, while remaining the point of contact for its own work. Other researchers or providers with access to confidential project information require appropriate prior arrangements. Rights in source and third-party material remain with their owners; delivery of a report does not grant unlimited rights to it.
11. **Report and use.** Unless otherwise agreed in writing, after payment the client receives a non-exclusive internal-use licence to the delivered report for the agreed business decision. External distribution, publication or use as an expert opinion requires prior written permission, subject to source and third-party rights. Pre-existing methods, formats and general know-how remain GFNI's property.
12. **Corrections and complaints.** The client identifies a concrete error or deficiency with reference to the report and underlying facts within a reasonable time after discovery. GFNI examines it and corrects demonstrable errors or proposes a suitable supplement. Disagreement with a clearly labelled inference is not automatically an error.
13. **Liability.** GFNI remains liable under applicable law for attributable failures. Any proportionate limitation, exceptions and treatment of indirect loss need legal review against the entity's form, actual risks and any insurance. This draft does not exclude all liability and invents no insured amount or cap.
14. **Force majeure, law and disputes.** For events outside reasonable control, parties discuss effects on delivery and timing without removing statutory rights. Dutch law and a suitable dispute route are envisaged, but final wording and the competent court require review against the entity and client type. Parties first try to resolve disputes by discussion.

**To decide before sending to clients:** verified full entity/trade name and address, VAT ID and price presentation, payment period and invoicing point, cancellation costs, cure period, liability, competent court, document version and delivery in a durable format. The scope confirmation must expressly refer to the approved version.
