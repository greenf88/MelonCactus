# Retention schedule for owner approval and operation

This is a policy choice for MelonCactus, not a claim that software currently deletes records automatically. It must be checked against the actual mailbox, project storage and backup arrangements before the privacy notice is published.

| Record | Proposed maximum in active systems | Reason / trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enquiry not converted to an assignment, including correspondence | 12 months after last substantive contact; sooner if follow-up is no longer needed | Limited B2B follow-up and accountability. Owner-approved on 1 October 2026. |
| Research working files | 24 months after final delivery; sooner if no longer needed | Corrections and proportionate review of the evidence. Owner-approved on 1 October 2026. |
| Final report | 5 years after final delivery | Proportionate record of what was delivered for corrections and contractual disputes. Chosen on 1 October 2026 as a policy maximum, **not** a universal statutory retention period. |
| Scope confirmation and records required for financial administration | Applicable statutory period, generally 7 years in the Netherlands | Keep the records actually needed to substantiate the transaction; do not apply this to every research file or unsuccessful enquiry. |
| Specific records subject to a concrete claim or another legal duty | Only as long as that separate need persists | Record the reason and review date; do not put the entire dossier on indefinite hold. |

**Backup decision:** use a target maximum of **90 days** for backup copies under MelonCactus's control after removal from active storage. This is a conservative operational choice, not a statutory period or a verified fact about any provider. It must not be represented on the website as an existing guarantee until the owner has identified the storage products, documented their backup rotation and tested deletion. Provider-held copies may follow different contractual schedules; obtain those schedules first. Restrict backup access to recovery, and if a backup is restored, reapply recorded deletions. Assess erasure requests against backups as well as active stores.

**Operating checklist before publication:** identify each active store and provider; record the person responsible for monthly expiry reviews; maintain a restricted deletion log containing dossier number, record category, deadline and completion date but no enquiry text; delete or anonymise expired active records; confirm mailbox trash/archive and device synchronisation; verify backup rotation and restore/deletion procedure; document provider retention and exceptions. No client data or live deletion log belongs in this repository.

The [Dutch Data Protection Authority](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/nl/over-privacy/persoonsgegevens/bewaren-van-persoonsgegevens) gives no universal GDPR retention period and requires a justified, documented choice. Its [erasure guidance](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/nl/zelf-doen/privacyrechten/recht-op-vergetelheid) includes backups. [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/administratie-bijhouden-en-bewaren/) describes a general seven-year duty for basic fiscal records. These sources do not certify this schedule or the still-unverified implementation.
